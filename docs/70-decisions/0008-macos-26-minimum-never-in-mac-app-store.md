# 0008 macOS 26 minimum, never in the Mac App Store

## Context

The App Sandbox would block the Steam folder and Discord's socket.
The decision was recorded without a separate reason for the minimum version.

## Decision

The app needs macOS 26 at least, and is never published in the Mac App Store.

## Alternatives considered

- The Mac App Store: the App Sandbox would block the Steam folder and Discord's socket.
