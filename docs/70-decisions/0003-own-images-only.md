# 0003 Every published image is our own

## Context

Third-party artwork may come with terms that are unclear.

## Decision

Every published image is our own: in-game shots of our savegame.
The cover, the app and tray icons and the Mod Hub images come from the game's photo mode.

## Consequences

- No third-party artwork whose terms are unclear is published.
